
# Adding products, services and goods

## Company product categories

Add categories to group your company's products, goods or services. A category
can have a name and an optional description. Categories are optional: a product
can be saved without one. You can also add **Price Rules** to a category using
the inline section on its edit page. These rules define how product price tiers
are created for products in the category. First add the required price rules to
the category's department; only tiers already configured for that department
can be selected in the category rules.

`(ADMIN) Home > Crm > Product categories`

## Company products

Add your company's products, services or goods. Open **Products** and create a
record for each item:

- Enter its name and choose whether it is a **Good** or a **Service**.
- Optionally select a product category and add a description.
- Optionally upload a main image and attach related files.

Product categories help organize the product list and can also be used when
configuring category-specific price rules. Product prices are managed as price
tiers, not in the product's basic details. Configure the department's price
rules first, then add price tiers to each product. See
[Product pricing](pricing_framework.md) for details.

`(ADMIN) Home > Crm > Products`
